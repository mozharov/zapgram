Support the project (optional)

ZapGram is free to use. You can optionally support the bot author with sats — never required, always under your control.

Voluntary % on payments

New accounts default to 5% on top of tips only. Invoice payments are excluded unless you switch the scope to all payments. Change the percent, scope, or turn it off in /settings → Support the project.

Your main tip or invoice pay settles first. The support tip is best-effort and never blocks the payment. Success is silent; failure only notifies you in private chat.

Monetize private chats with sats

Turn a private group or channel into a sat-gated community — Lightning and optional on-chain Bitcoin. No Stripe, no bank account.

For owners

  1. Add @zap_gram_bot with invite and ban rights.
  2. Open /chats → enable paid access.
  3. Set price in sats: one-time permanent access or monthly subscription. Optional join message in RU + EN.
  4. Optional: enable on-chain pay and paste your account zpub / xpub — members can pay on-chain; funds go to your wallet.

Lightning joins: owners are paid automatically (minus a small platform fee on the hosted bot). On-chain joins: sats go straight to your key — no Lightning fee split. Duplicate Lightning payments are refunded safely.

For members

  1. Request to join.
  2. Get a Lightning invoice (and one-tap pay if your ZapGram or NWC balance covers it), or Pay on-chain when the owner enabled it.
  3. Pay either way → access is granted.

Monthly renewals auto-debit via Lightning from the ZapGram wallet. Manage status in /subscriptions. For expiry reminders, open the bot with /start.

Optional self-custody with NWC

Prefer keys you control? Connect any Lightning wallet that supports Nostr Wallet Connect — the same protocol Nostriches already use for zaps.

  • Link a nostr+walletconnect://… URL
  • See both balances (ZapGram + NWC) on the wallet screen
  • Choose which wallet pays tips in groups
  • Pay some invoices and subscriptions from NWC when balance allows
  • Disconnect anytime

Your external wallet stays under your control. ZapGram only acts within the permissions you grant.

Frequently asked questions

What is ZapGram?

ZapGram is a Bitcoin Lightning wallet inside Telegram. It lets you send and receive sats, tip in groups and channels, pay BOLT11 invoices, and sell paid access to private chats — without leaving the messenger.

Is it a wallet or just a tip bot?

Both. You get a full Lightning wallet on first open, plus /tip in groups, invoices with QR, username transfers, and sat-gated chats (Lightning and optional on-chain) with subscriptions.

Is ZapGram free? What are the fees?

Free to start. Internal transfers between ZapGram wallets are free. External Lightning payments pay network fees. On the hosted bot, paid-chat owner payouts take a small platform fee (default 5%). Optional support for the bot author (voluntary % on tips/invoice pays, or /donate) is separate and can be turned off in settings.

Can I support the ZapGram project with sats?

Yes. New accounts include a voluntary 5% on top of tips only; invoice payments are excluded by default. Change or turn it off in /settings. Use /donate for one-shot or monthly support, personal and community totals, or send to zapgram@getalby.com. This never blocks your main payment.

Are my sats custodial?

The built-in wallet is custodial convenience. Prefer self-custody? Connect an NWC wallet (e.g. Alby, Coinos), tip and pay from that balance, disconnect anytime. Source is MIT open source.

Is ZapGram safe to use? How do I know it is legitimate?

ZapGram is open source under the MIT license on GitHub — you can read the code or self-host. Opening the bot needs no card and no bank account. The built-in wallet is custodial convenience; connect NWC for self-custody. It is not a bank and not financial advice.

What happens when I open the bot?

You open @zap_gram_bot in Telegram, tap Start, and a Lightning wallet is created. No separate app install. Fund later with a receive invoice from any Lightning wallet, or connect NWC when you want self-custody.

What is NWC and why support it?

Nostr Wallet Connect lets apps request payments from a wallet you control. ZapGram can show both balances and use your NWC wallet for tips and some payments — familiar if you already zap on Nostr.

How do tips work in groups?

Add the bot. /tip sends 21 sats to the owner; /tip 100 sets an amount; reply + /tip tips the author; /tip @user or /tip 50 @user targets a person.

Can I sell access to a private group or channel with Bitcoin?

Yes. In /chats, enable paid access, set a sat price (one-time or monthly), grant invite + ban rights. Members pay Lightning and/or on-chain if you enable on-chain pay with a zpub/xpub. Access is granted after payment.

How is ZapGram different from LightningTipBot or other tip bots?

ZapGram combines wallet, tips, BOLT11 invoices, paid chats with subscriptions, optional NWC, and optional project support (/donate) in one bot. Many peers focus mainly on tipping or simple send/receive. Compare features — the Telegram Lightning category is still small.

Do I need a separate app or Lightning node?

No. Open @zap_gram_bot, fund with a receive invoice from any Lightning wallet, or attach NWC. No node required for normal use.

Is ZapGram open source? Can I self-host?

Yes — MIT on GitHub. Self-host if you want your own instance; most people just open the hosted bot. Open source is for trust and auditability.

What languages does the bot support?

Russian and English UI. Other Telegram languages fall back to English. This site: English (canonical) and Russian (/ru).

Is this the same as Telegram Wallet (@wallet)?

No. @wallet is a multi-asset product. ZapGram is Lightning-native: sats, BOLT11, tips, and sat-gated chats.

Where is ZapGram used in the real world?

One live partner is 21ideas — a community with Bitcoin learning materials. In the 21ideas community ZapGram is used for tips in chat and paid access. Channel: @bitcoin21ideas. ZapGram guide from 21ideas.
